Vehicles and museums

I have selected four vehicles I enjoy studying: two tanks, an anti-aircraft vehicle and a fighter. Enlarge the photographs and expand each card to learn why they interest me.

  • Why it interests me

    With the T-90M, I enjoy looking at how protection, mobility and armament are combined. Comparing it with other tanks helps me understand its development.
