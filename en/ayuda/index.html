How to use this website

Press Tab to move to the next link or control, and Shift + Tab to go back. Activate them with Enter; buttons also support Space. The first link lets you skip to the main content.

In carousels, use the buttons, swipe, or press the left and right arrow keys when the carousel is focused. They do not advance automatically. In enlarged photographs, use Close image or Escape to return.

The menu indicates your current section. You can switch between Spanish and English in the header.

How to search

Enter one or more words in the header search field and press Search or Enter. Accents and capital letters do not change the results. When you enter several words, results must contain all of them.

If you cannot find what you need, try fewer words or use the site map. If loading fails, press Retry search. Without JavaScript, you can still use the menu and site map links.

Something not working?

If audio or video does not play, use its download link. If a link is broken or you need content in another format, email uo294228@uniovi.es with the page address and a description of the problem.

Adjust reading to your preferences

Press the floating Accessibility button in the bottom corner of any page. A panel opens with text up to 200%, the OpenDyslexic font for dyslexia, spacing, simple headings, highlighted links and reduced motion. Try the settings and choose what feels comfortable. OpenDyslexic is optional and applies across the website; it takes priority over the simple heading font. Close settings or Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the button. Reset settings restores the original design and removes saved preferences.

Settings are optional, saved locally and shared across pages and both languages in this browser. They are not sent to any service. If storage is blocked, they work for the current page visit. Without JavaScript, you can still navigate and use browser zoom and accessibility features.
